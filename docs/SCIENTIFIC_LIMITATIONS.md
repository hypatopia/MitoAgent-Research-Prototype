# Scientific and product limitations

- This repository is a research prototype, not a medical device.
- It does not diagnose Alzheimer’s disease or any other disease.
- OCR traces alone cannot establish isolated Complex IV dysfunction.
- The reduced model does not reconstruct the full electron transport chain.
- Parameter estimates must be interpreted together with identifiability and
  numerical-stability diagnostics.
- Demonstration datasets are synthetic or representative fixtures.
- Hosted LLM functionality is optional and does not perform numerical inference.
- The prototype is not the commercial adaptive digital-twin platform proposed
  by the founder.
